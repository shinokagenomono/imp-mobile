IMP Master – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

IMP Master and its mobile web app (IMP Mobile) are a personal tool built and used by a single developer to manage his own notes and tasks. This page describes how the app handles data.

What the app accesses

After you sign in with Google, the app reads and writes files in one Google Drive folder named "IMP Master" (notes and task lists). The desktop version can additionally read Gmail message headers and Google Calendar events of the signed-in account to show reminders.

Where data goes

All data stays between your Google account and your own device. The app has no server and no database of its own. It does not send, sell, share or publish any data to the developer or any third party.

Storage on your device

The mobile app keeps a short-lived Google access token in the browser's session storage until the tab is closed. The desktop version stores its sign-in token and settings in local files on the user's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
